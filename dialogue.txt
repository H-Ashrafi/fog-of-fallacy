FOG OF FALLACY — EVERY LINE OF DIALOGUE

Notes for whoever rewrites this file:
* Keep every [key] exactly as it is, one line per key, so the new text can be put back in the game.
* The players are young children (about 7). Use short, simple, whole sentences. Every sentence needs a verb.
* {name} is replaced by the player's name. Keep it wherever it appears.
* PLAYER ANSWER lines are the choices on screen. The ✓ RIGHT answer must never be the longest one,
  or children learn to pick the longest. Wrong answers should sound tempting and reasonable, not silly.
  ✗ INSULT answers are name-calling on purpose: they teach that insults are not arguments.
* Each argument teaches the named trick (fallacy). The bad argument must still clearly use that trick.
* Numbers of coins in spoken lines must match the game (they are shown in the HUD too).
* NARRATOR lines are read by a storyteller. SIMURGH is a wise, warm, ancient magic bird.
